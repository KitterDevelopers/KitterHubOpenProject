# Code of Conduct

> 中文版: [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our pledge

This project is a community for young creators. Everyone taking part — maintainers, contributors, and members — is expected to help make it a place where people can be wrong, ask basic questions, and still be treated with respect.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and with people writing in a second language.
- Give technical criticism about the code, never about the person.
- Assume good faith; ask a clarifying question before assuming malice.
- Respect a maintainer's "no" and move on.
- Keep other people's private information private — no screenshots, logs, or real names in public threads.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate provocation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a space that teenagers also use. If you have to ask whether it crosses that line, assume it does.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, commit messages, discussions — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abuse to the maintainers (see the contact details in [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md)). Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including warnings, removing comments, or banning accounts from the project's spaces. Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face the same consequences.
